    FAQ

    How is this different from cal.com or Calendly?

    Calendars schedule future slots. Free Right Now answers a live question: is now a good time? It is a complement to scheduling, not a replacement for long-term planning.

    Do I have to share everything?

    No. Each signal is optional. The app gets smarter as you connect more signals, but you stay in control.

    Does it read my messages or code?

    No. It only uses presence and activity signals like "active", "idle", or "last seen".

    How does it learn my rules?

    The daily review confirms what was right or wrong. Over time the model learns your personal availability patterns.
